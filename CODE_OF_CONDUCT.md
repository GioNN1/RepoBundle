# Code of Conduct

RepoBundle contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, and observable behavior rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that materially disrupt a safe and productive project environment. Serious concerns should be raised privately with the repository maintainers whenever possible.
